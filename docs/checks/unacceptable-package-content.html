unacceptable-package-content

What it detects

This is a human-review item with no automated logic behind it. A reviewer inspects what the add-on ships: its name and description in manifest.json, its icons, and any text, images or media bundled in the package.

Each is weighed for spam, inappropriate, misleading or low-effort content, and against Mozilla's Acceptable Use Policy. Everything judged here lives inside the submitted package, so every fix needs a new version. The ATN listing is judged separately by unacceptable-listing-content.

When it fires The developer is told that content shipped inside the add-on does not meet our content requirements, and is asked to correct it and submit a new version.

Source of truth: the manual-checks entry in assets/registry.yaml.
