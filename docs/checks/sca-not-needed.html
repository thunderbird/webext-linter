sca-not-needed

What it reports

An add-on submitted with a source-code archive it did not need: its source review was restarted as a review of the XPI alone, and the developer is told to submit just the XPI next time.

Nothing in the files decides it, so the check raises nothing itself. Under --llm-review, a source review aborted only by checks a review of the XPI alone cures (review-downgrade in the registry), whose XPI holds no minified, obfuscated or untrusted minified code, is asked in the downgrade phase whether to restart as an XPI review. Answering Restart as an XPI review starts that review in a work folder of its own, and it reports this check. A plain XPI review never does.

When it fires The developer is told that the add-on was submitted with a source-code archive, that its XPI could be reviewed on its own and so was reviewed without the archive, and that submitting just the XPI next time is reviewed considerably faster.

Source of truth: src/checks/rules/sca-not-needed.js, the finding added in src/pipeline.js, the downgrade phase and the entry in assets/registry.yaml.
